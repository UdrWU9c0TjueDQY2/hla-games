USE IT IN REAL LIFE

Read the unit before reading the digits.

The convention used here

Suffix Meaning in this tool Multiplier
K Thousand 1,000
M Million 1,000,000
B Billion 1,000,000,000
T Trillion 1,000,000,000,000

This uses the short-scale convention. Not every game or language uses these exact labels. Check the game’s own notation; unsupported suffixes are rejected rather than guessed.

Three comparisons worth understanding

  • 950K is smaller than 1.2M. Expand them to 950,000 and 1,200,000. The difference is 250,000.
  • 1.5B and 1500M are equal here. Both expand to 1,500,000,000. A longer string of digits does not necessarily mean a bigger value.
  • 1.000001T differs from 1T by a million. A tiny change in the displayed coefficient can represent a large absolute quantity.

A rounded label is not an exact balance

A label such as 1.2M may be an abbreviated display. Without knowing the game’s rounding rule and hidden digits, you cannot prove that two matching labels represent equal stored values. This tool compares the text you enter; it cannot recover information the label leaves out.

Why the bars sometimes look equal

The bars show relative size at limited screen resolution. A small gap can disappear visually even when the exact difference is nonzero. Read the expanded values and difference for that case. The ratio is rounded to three decimal places and marked approximate.
