THE METHOD, WITHOUT THE MYSTERY

Why does 8 + 7 become 10 + 5?

1. Find the gap.

Eight needs two more to make ten: 10 − 8 = 2.

2. Split the seven.

Take two from the seven. Five remain: 7 = 2 + 5.

3. Regroup.

Combine eight and two, then add the five: 8 + 7 = 8 + 2 + 5 = 10 + 5 = 15.

The colors keep track of where the counters started. Blue counters belong to the first number; orange counters belong to the second. Moving orange counters changes their position, not the total.

A few useful distinctions.

What is the make-ten strategy?

It is a way to regroup an addition so one part becomes ten. For 9 + 6, move one from the six: 9 + 1 + 5 = 10 + 5 = 15. The total stays the same.

What if my numbers total less than ten?

The tool puts the counters together and shows the total, but there is no full ten to bridge through. Try 8 + 7 for an example with a remainder.

Is this a test or a score?

No. You can step forward and backward, change numbers and look again. There is no timer, grade or stored performance record.

Does it save anything or need an account?

No account or browser storage is used. The copy buttons write the equation or a link containing only your two selected numbers when you choose them. Shared links include no name, score or account. Print uses your browser’s print controls; nothing is uploaded by the worksheet.
