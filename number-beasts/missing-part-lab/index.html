TABLETOP PUZZLE

Make-ten link cards

Start with tile F. Match its right number to the left number on the next tile so the two add to 10. Use every tile once and keep them upright. You can cut out the cards or solve by writing their letters in order.

Read all seven tiles on screen
  • Tile A: 9 on the left, 5 on the right.
  • Tile B: 4 on the left, 3 on the right.
  • Tile C: 3 on the left, end on the right.
  • Tile D: 2 on the left, 1 on the right.
  • Tile E: 5 on the left, 7 on the right.
  • Tile F: start on the left, 6 on the right.
  • Tile G: 7 on the left, 8 on the right.

Bonus: If G ends in 4 instead of 8, which number must start D so the new join still makes ten?

Check the chain and bonus answer

F → B → G → D → A → E → C. The joins are 6 + 4, 3 + 7, 8 + 2, 1 + 9, 5 + 5 and 7 + 3. In the bonus, D starts with 6 because 4 + 6 = 10.

MAKE THE RESULT USEFUL

Use the whole to find the gap.

If the whole is 10 and one part is 6, count from 6 up to 10: four steps. That gives both 6 + 4 = 10 and 10 − 6 = 4.

  1. Say the whole first.
  2. Look at the part you already know.
  3. Find what is left, then add the two parts to check.

The later rounds change the whole to 15 and 20. The relationship stays the same.
