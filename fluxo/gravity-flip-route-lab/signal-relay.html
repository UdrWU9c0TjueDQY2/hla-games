Signal Relay

Two players share one screen. The navigator studies eight gates and talks the pilot through them. The pilot programs the route with the map hidden. Switch jobs on the next course.

Navigator, read the course

    Agree on a short message, such as “flip at gate two, then stay until gate five.” Hand over the screen after hiding the map. You can also play alone from memory.

    Pilot, program your route

    Stay keeps your lane. Flip switches lanes before entering the next gate. Enter one command per gate, using the navigator’s message.

    Flight log

      Compare the first unexpected lane with the navigator’s message. Which instruction would you change?

      Made for clubs, classrooms and pairs. Save this HTML file and reopen it in a browser. The whole relay works offline, with no account or saved player information.
