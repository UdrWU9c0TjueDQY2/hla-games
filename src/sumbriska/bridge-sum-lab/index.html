THE IDEA, IN ONE MOVE

You’re moving value.
Not adding more.

If a bridge gives 1 to island A and 4 to island B, a flip gives 4 to A and 1 to B. A gains 3. B loses 3. The total stays 5.

That’s the useful trick: look at what both ends need. A move that fixes one island can disturb another.

BEFORE YOUR NEXT FLIP

A few good questions.

Are these number puzzles for adults?

Anyone who enjoys a quiet arithmetic puzzle can try this sampler. It starts gently and adds connected decisions. You only need addition, comparison and a little patience.

Is this the same as Hashi or a bridges puzzle?

Traditional Hashi asks you to place bridges between islands. Here, bridges already exist: you flip the numbers at their ends to match island sums. The shared island theme does not make the rules identical.

Do I have to download anything to play?

No. All six practice boards work here in a modern browser with JavaScript enabled. The separate Sumbriska iPhone and iPad app is available through the App Store.

Does the sampler save or track me?

The sampler keeps its progress in this open page only. Refreshing clears it. This page has no added analytics, ads, accounts or forms. The website host may process routine connection logs.

Is a hint a penalty?

No. Try a hint, use Undo or select any board. This sampler has no score, clock or penalty for exploring.
