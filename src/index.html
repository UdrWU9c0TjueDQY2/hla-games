Puffgrove: Grove Creatures

A little grove. A creature with its own habits.

Hatch a creature, arrange its grove and help shape its habits through play. Puffgrove combines a cozy habitat with Toy Trail puzzles and Sniff Out treat hunts.

View Puffgrove on the App Store

The Grove, Toy Trail and Sniff Out are free. Optional Plus purchases unlock more farm, town and journey features; optional gem packs are also available. See the App Store for current local prices.
