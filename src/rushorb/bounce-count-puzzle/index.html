How this bouncing-ball puzzle works

The track has positions 0 through 6. Each step moves the orb to the neighboring position. At 0 or 6, its next step travels inward. There is no pause step, speed change or gravity in this simplified game.

One worked example

Start at 5 moving right and take four steps: 6, 5, 4, 3. The answer is 3. Count moves, not the starting position. You can reveal the full route after finding the answer.

Watch the first rebound (12 seconds)

Round one starts at 2. Five steps to the right land on 3, 4, 5, 6, then 5. The fifth move heads left because 6 is the end of the track.

A useful shortcut for longer routes

A complete trip from one end to the other and back takes 12 steps. Twelve extra steps therefore returns the orb to the same position. Use this only for this seven-position model; it is not a rule for RushOrb physics.

Play at your own pace

There is no timer or leaderboard. Wrong selections stay disabled for the current puzzle. The recap records first-choice answers separately from completed puzzles. Keyboard users can Tab to a position and press Enter.
