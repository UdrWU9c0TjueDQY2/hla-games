Show the worked answer

Narrowest bridge case 1

A single route crosses three bridges with load limits 8, 5, 7 counters. A traveler must carry the same whole load across every bridge without unloading. A load equal to a limit is allowed. What is the greatest load that can complete the route?

Show the worked answer

B. 5

The route can carry at most 5 counters, the smallest bridge limit. That load fits all three bridges. Any larger load fails at least one bridge, regardless of how generous the other bridge limits are.

Show the worked answer

Avoid the closed station case 3

Three routes are listed: S→A→T, S→B→T and S→C→T. Station C is closed, so a route visiting it is forbidden. Every other listed route is open. Which option gives all the usable routes?

C. S→A→T and S→B→T

S→A→T and S→B→T remain usable because neither visits C. Remove the route containing the closed station, but keep the others. Sharing the same start and exit does not make their intermediate stations identical.
