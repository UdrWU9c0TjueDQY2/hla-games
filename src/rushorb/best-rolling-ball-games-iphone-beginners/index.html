    3. Beginner questions

      Is RushOrb hard for a beginner?

      The listing describes one-thumb steering, hand-built stages with a finish gate, and three sparks per stage where only the first (reaching the gate) is needed to move on. So you can finish a stage badly and still progress; the beacon and challenge sparks are for coming back later. We have not seen a difficulty setting mentioned.

      Do I need two hands?

      You steer with one thumb. Calling a weather (Frost, Blaze, Gale) is a tap on the dial, which the listing describes as the other thumb; on a phone most people will do both with one hand in short runs and two when a stage gets busy.

      Does it work offline?

      Yes, per the listing: no ads, no account, full offline play. Game Center leaderboards and the Daily challenge's shared seed will need a connection when you want to sync.

      Does the subscription block any stages?

      The listing says the optional cosmetic packs and RushOrb Plus are for style and bonus rewards, never for clearing stages, and coins are earned only by playing. Plus is an auto-renewing subscription; read the terms before tapping.

      What is the Weather Dial?

      A three-way switch. Frost freezes ghost bridges solid and locks you onto rails; Blaze melts glass gates and runs faster; Gale lightens the orb and stretches jumps. Storm Walls only part for the weather they are painted with, which is the whole puzzle.

      Is it OK for kids?

      Rated 4+ with no ads and no account per the listing. There is an optional subscription, so set Screen Time purchase limits if a child has your phone.
