MAKE THE RESULT USEFUL

A purchase count is also a price sequence.

At 20% growth, five prices starting at 10 are 10, 12, 14.40, 17.28 and 20.74 fictional credits. The total is 74.42, not 50.

Set growth to zero to see the flat-price case. Then change only the purchase count: the early steps stay the same, while the later steps extend the ladder.
