Make Ten Addition Lab — 8 + 7
Honest Little Apps | Captioned silent web activity demonstration | 18 seconds

0:00–0:05
The activity shows 8 blue counters in the first ten-frame and 7 orange counters in the second. Ask: “How many spaces are empty in the blue frame?”

0:05–0:11
After “Move the counters,” 2 orange counters fill the empty spots beside the 8 blue counters. Ask: “How many orange counters stayed in the second frame?” Five remain.

0:11–0:18
After “Show the total,” the activity displays 10 + 5 = 15. Ask: “What moved? What stayed the same?” Two counters moved between frames. All 15 counters are still on the board.
