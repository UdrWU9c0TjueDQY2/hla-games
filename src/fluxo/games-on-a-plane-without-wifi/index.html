  20. Questions from the same chat

  21. Honest Little Apps
    Does Fluxo work in airplane mode?

    Per the App Store listing: no account, no login, no internet required. The run, the three modes, the pilots and the missions are on the phone. The two things that need a connection are the share link for daring a friend and the optional watch-an-ad revive, which will not load without signal, so you get a plain Retry.

    What do I need to do before boarding?

    Download it on the ground (about 15 MB per the listing, iOS 18 or newer), open it once so the first-run TAP TO FLIP coaching is done, then switch to airplane mode. That is the whole list.

    Is it free?

    Yes. The listing says the full game is playable free, with house-ad breaks in the free version and a one-time optional premium (2x gold, free revives, no house-ad breaks, the Golden Fluxo pilot). Never a subscription.

    Is there a story or levels to finish?

    No. It is an endless one-touch runner: every 8 points you enter a new sector with its own rule, and a Daily Challenge gives one fixed course per day. If you want chapters and an ending, this is the wrong app and we say so below.

    Is it OK for kids on the flight?

    The App Store rating is 4+ and there is no chat or account. There are optional purchases and ad prompts in the free version, so set Screen Time purchase limits if a child has your phone.

    Will it make me motion sick in a window seat?

    It is fast by design. The listing lists toggles for screen shake, reduced effects and haptics, and it honors iOS Reduce Motion; Zen mode has no wipeouts. If you are prone to motion sickness in the air, switch those on before you start, or pick something slower.
