    One more piece of evidence

    NASA puts the first clear TV picture at 02:54:00 UTC and Armstrong’s first step at 02:56:15 UTC. How much time passed?

    Read the answer key and check the sources

    All five entries are on July 21 in UTC. The hatch opened at 02:39:33. Armstrong was fully outside on the porch at 02:51:16. The first clear TV picture arrived at 02:54:00. His first step followed at 02:56:15, and collection of the contingency sample began at 03:05:58. A clear picture arriving and a person setting foot on the surface are separate events.

    The record slips paraphrase NASA’s Apollo 11 timeline. Its mission summary also gives the first step as July 20 at 10:56 p.m. US Eastern daylight time. That is the same instant as July 21 at 02:56 UTC. The times on the slips are recorded events, distinct from planned times in a flight plan.
